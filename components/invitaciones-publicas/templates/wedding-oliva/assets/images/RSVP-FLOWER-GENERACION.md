## Prompt completo

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate a NEW reusable botanical PNG mask asset for the attendance section of a refined olive-green and beige wedding invitation.
Reference image 1 (gold wax seal) is a shape/style reference ONLY: use the central botanical arrangement inside the seal, especially its compact upright flower, branching stems, buds and almond-shaped leaves. Reference image 2 (white MS monogram) is a supporting reference ONLY for delicate botanical linework; do not copy letters.
Create ONE small, elegant, cohesive flowering sprig: a central five-petalled wildflower, two gently outward-curving leafy stems and a few small buds, with the overall silhouette like the floral motif in the gold seal. Organic and balanced, slightly asymmetrical, compact nearly square proportions, not a long garland. The botanical design fills approximately 78% of a square canvas; all tips fully visible with generous clear margin. Delicate engraved-looking contours with readable medium-thin strokes and a few white solid petal/leaf accents, enough transparent interior negative space to show veins. Simplify tiny details so it remains legible at about 96 CSS pixels wide.
IMPORTANT OUTPUT: pure WHITE (#FFFFFF) botanical strokes and shapes on a genuinely TRANSPARENT alpha background. White only, no other colors, no gray shading. Flat mask artwork: NO baked embossing, depth, lighting, gradients, material texture, glow, shadow or paper; the website will apply its own embossed relief with CSS. Do not include the circular seal, outer rim, wax disk, wording ABRIR, initials MS, any text, border, background rectangle or watermark. No checkerboard painted in the image. This is finished transparent botanical artwork, not a mockup.
